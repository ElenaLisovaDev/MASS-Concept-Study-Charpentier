# Maritime Autonomous Surface System (MASS) Concept Study

## Agentic Engineering for Uncrewed Surface Vessel (USV) Design, Regulatory Analysis, and Digital Development

### Welcome

Welcome to the 2026-2027 Maritime Autonomous Surface System (MASS) Concept Study.

You are part of a multidisciplinary engineering team at: **Charpentier Shipyard**

As a team, you will develop a concept-level **Uncrewed Surface Vessel (USV)** using a modern **Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE)** approach supported by **AI-driven Agentic Engineering** workflows.

The objective is not simply to design a vessel. The objective is to create a technically credible, operationally useful, and regulatorily compliant autonomous maritime system while exploring how AI agents can augment engineering processes from requirements analysis through virtual prototyping.

## "Design the System. Understand the Rules. Augment the Engineer."

**Charpentier Shipyard** is challenged to demonstrate how the next generation of maritime autonomous systems can be conceived through the combination of systems engineering, digital design, and AI-enabled engineering practices.

---

# Project Vision

Maritime autonomy is rapidly transforming both civil and defense domains. Future operators, shipbuilders, authorities, and technology providers must navigate increasing technical complexity while simultaneously addressing international regulations, safety requirements, interoperability demands, and emerging autonomous system standards.

This project demonstrates how **AI Agents**, aligned with SysML models, engineering artifacts, and regulatory knowledge domains, can accelerate:

- Requirements derivation
- Operational concept development
- System architecture design
- Regulatory analysis
- Design space exploration
- Human-machine interface development
- Traceability between requirements and implementation

By integrating engineering and policy considerations from the start, the project aims to create a repeatable workflow suitable for future defense, maritime authority, and industrial applications.

---

# Project Challenge

Design a virtual concept of a Maritime Autonomous Surface System (MASS) and demonstrate how agentic engineering can support the end-to-end concept development process.

The project shall integrate:

1. **Regulatory Analysis and Requirements Engineering**
2. **MBSE Modeling and System Architecture**
3. **Virtual Prototyping and Remote Control UI Development**

The resulting concept should:

- Address realistic maritime operational needs
- Demonstrate autonomous and remotely supervised operations
- Maintain traceability from regulations to system requirements
- Utilize SysML-based system architecture models
- Include a representative digital prototype and operator interface
